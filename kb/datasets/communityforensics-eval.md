# Community Forensics Eval (CompEval)
- **Source/URL:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/OwensLab/CommunityForensics-Eval · **Paper:** communityforensics-cvpr2025 · **License:** cc-by-nc-sa-4.0 (card/API) -> **NON-COMMERCIAL**; the card says non-commercial research and educational use only, cite RAISE, COCO, FFHQ, LAION; a RAISE_License.pdf is included.
- **Size:** 51,836 examples, split CompEval; download_size 206,224,324,595 bytes (~206 GB) in 413 parquet shards (card metadata). Full download is impossible under 5 GB.
- **Real sources:** RAISE, COCO, FFHQ, LAION. **Generators:** 21 held-out (11 commercial: DALL-E 2/3, Ideogram, Midjourney V5/V6, Firefly 2/3, FLUX.1 dev/schnell, Imagen 3; others incl. Kandinsky 2.2, Stable Cascade, LCM-LoRA variants, GALIP, DF-GAN, HDiT) - from the paper (Sec. on evaluation set).
- **Formats:** Parquet with fields format, resolution, image_data bytes, model_name, real_source, label; the card example shows PNG. Per-source format mix (JPEG reals from COCO/LAION vs PNG fakes) NOT verified -> check the `format` column before use; **format-bias risk is real** because COCO/LAION reals are JPEG originals.
- **Download procedure:** `datasets.load_dataset("OwensLab/CommunityForensics-Eval", split="CompEval", streaming=True)` streams without a full download; or fetch individual parquet files by name (listing via HF API: shards range from ~5 MB to ~2.9 GB each; I did not map shards to generators). Filter on `model_name` / `real_source`.
- **Known issues:** card says its metrics are within +/-0.001 of the paper (mAP 0.987 vs 0.987; mAcc 0.893 vs 0.892 for Ours-384). Use the provided FFHQ train/test split to avoid contamination. Reals are not phone photos; RAISE reals are DSLR.
- **Used in our experiments?** no (candidate; stream <=5 GB sample)
