# Self-referenced decoder-trace contrast: score how far an image moves along the learned VAE-trace direction when it is round-tripped through the VAE, with both sides passed through the same canonical laundering
- **id:** self-ref-trace-contrast
- **Gap addressed:** laundering fragility of decoder traces (E4); content variance swamping a weak trace; real-photo FPR of reconstruction scores
- **Scores (1-5):** novelty 3 · expected gain on unseen/in-the-wild 4 · feasibility 5 · **product 60** (rank 1 in docs/06-diffusion-hypotheses.md)

- **Hypothesis:** For a test image t, the shift S(t) = w · [phi(L0(VAE(t))) − phi(L0(t))] (phi = frozen DINOv2-base blocks 4/8/12 CLS+mean, w = the trace direction learned only from real-vs-own-round-trip pairs, L0 = canonical jpeg75_resize05 applied to BOTH sides) is large for real photos and small for diffusion images, because a decoded image is (nearly) a fixed point of a VAE round-trip in the mid-frequency band that survives laundering; this self-referenced score reaches macro AUROC >= 0.80 at jpeg75_resize05 on 6 unseen Synthbuster generators (fov512 arm), i.e. >= +0.10 over the pooled pair-trained probe of E4.

- **Mechanism (why diffusion images differ):**
  - Latent diffusion images are decoder outputs. Re-encoding a decoder output returns (almost) the same latent, so a second decode changes it little ("idempotence"); a real photo is not in the decoder's range, so the round-trip adds a fresh decoder rendering (AEROBLADE's premise, aeroblade-cvpr2024; HFI, hfi-arxiv2024).
  - Pixel-space versions of this score track texture and fail on our data: #7's VAE ratio got 0.601 clean / 0.318 laundered and lost to the Laplacian baseline (experiments/009-vae-aligned/pilot/tables.md via docs/05 §2). The new ingredients are (a) reading the shift only along a DINOv2 direction that E4 showed is trace-specific (beats texture by +0.31 orig / +0.41 fov512 clean, experiments/011-vae-trace/pilot/tables.md), and (b) a per-image content reference.
  - Why a reference should help under laundering: in E4 the pooled pair AUROC at jpeg75_resize05 is only 0.670 (orig) / 0.739 (fov512) with matched training, yet the WITHIN-pair win-rate is 0.980 in both arms (011/pilot/tables.md, "Pair AUROC, matched training", F2). The laundered trace is therefore still present but smaller than the between-image (content) spread. Subtracting a same-content reference removes that spread.
  - Why L0 on both sides: laundering breaks idempotence mostly in the fine band (the VAE re-synthesises fine detail that JPEG/resize removed, for reals and fakes alike). Passing both t and VAE(t) through the same L0 discards that band and keeps the mid band, where Cozzolino et al. (arXiv 2608.12155, abstract) locate the useful real-vs-diffusion cues.

- **Closest prior work:** aeroblade-cvpr2024 and hfi-arxiv2024 (pixel/LPIPS distance x vs AE(x), training-free; JPEG only in figures, no resize); fire-cvpr2025 (mid-band VAE reconstruction error, trained on generator data, 68-73% ACC on unseen newer generators, Table 2); rdd-cvpr2026 (debiased reconstruction error, abstract only); lare2-cvpr2024 (latent error); vae-double-recon-ratio (our dropped #7). Web: "A Difference-in-Difference Approach to Detecting AI-Generated Images" (arXiv 2602.23732; CVPR 2026 per search listing, abstract opened) uses a second-order difference of reconstruction errors to cut variance - the closest idea; its space, reconstruction model and laundering results are not stated in the abstract [unverified]. Differences here: feature-space projection on a direction learned from real pairs only (no generator images), canonical laundering applied to both sides, and evaluation under JPEG+resize with real-photo FPR, which none of the above reports.

- **Evidence for / against:**
  - For: within-pair win-rates 0.991 (orig) / 1.000 (fov512) clean and 0.980 laundered with matched training (011/pilot/tables.md); trace-specific vs texture at clean (+0.308 orig, +0.406 fov512); the E4 F2 direction already transfers to non-SD generators in fov512 clean (other-group 0.875) without ever seeing them.
  - Against: the win-rate is for L(VAE(x)) vs L(x) (VAE applied BEFORE laundering); at test time the VAE is applied AFTER laundering, which is a different quantity. Laundered fakes may lose idempotence entirely (then S is large for both classes). The matched probe transferred badly to laundered fakes (orig F2 0.494, fov512 0.626, 011 transfer table) - a warning that the laundered trace direction is SD-VAE-specific. Reconstruction detectors are white-box fragile (fragile-recon-arxiv2026, Fig. 3). Non-LDM generators (DALL-E 2) are weak for every AE score (hfi-arxiv2024 Table 4: 0.425 AUPR).

- **Test plan:**
  - *Pilot now (no download):* VISION (10 train devices, 297 pairs cached by 009/011) + 6 Synthbuster generators x 120, sd-vae-ft-mse on GPU (D-008 guard already passed), DINOv2-base. Arms `fov512` (label arm, per docs/05 §5a) and `orig`. Conditions clean / jpeg75_resize05 / social_sim.
    - Scores: A = w·[phi(VAE t) − phi(t)] (no L0); B = the L0 version above; A2 = a generator-free logistic probe on difference vectors, trained on real pairs: positives Delta1 = phi(VAE x) − phi(x), negatives Delta2 = phi(VAE(VAE x)) − phi(VAE x) (second round-trip as a proxy "already decoded" image). w = E4's primary F2 probe (clean pairs) for A; matched (laundered pairs) for B.
    - Baselines: pooled E4 F2 probe (transfer 0.892 / 0.665 fov512, 0.776 / 0.643 orig, 011 tables); pixel L2 round-trip error (AEROBLADE-like, no LPIPS download); Laplacian texture; B0 LOGO for reference.
    - New VAE passes: VAE(t) for 1,074 test reals and 720 fakes per arm per laundered condition, fakes clean, and VAE(VAE x) for 358 natives per arm: about 8.6k passes. At E4's measured 1,074 passes in 418-504 s (011/pilot/run.log) this is about 1 h GPU; DINOv2 features minutes.
  - *Validity gates (before reading AUROC):* (1) saturation: on unseen-device natives, median w·Delta2 <= 0.5 x median w·Delta1 at clean; otherwise the mechanism is absent and the result is UNINFORMATIVE. (2) Content cancellation: Spearman(w·phi(t), w·phi(VAE t)) >= 0.8 on reals. (3) Train-pair orientation fixed on train pairs, never flipped on test. (4) D-008 probe guard.
  - **Pass (pilot, fov512):** B macro AUROC >= 0.80 (CI lo >= 0.75) at jpeg75_resize05 AND >= pooled F2 + 0.10; >= 0.90 clean; >= 0.80 social_sim; non-SD group (DALL-E 2/3, Firefly, MJ5) >= 0.75 laundered; beats texture and pixel-L2 by >= 0.10 in every condition; FPR at a 1% threshold calibrated on held-out-device natives <= 2.0% on the other devices' natives, WA and FBH copies. `orig` reported with the same rules. **Fail:** B < 0.70 laundered or <= pooled F2 + 0.03. Between: inconclusive.
  - *Official:* held-out FLUX and SD3.5 (16-channel VAEs, not in the bank), RAISE PNG reals, >= 30 devices, B-Free/CommFor/RINE comparators; Phase-5 bar AUROC >= 0.95 laundered, phone FPR <= 1%.
  - *Weights:* sd-vae-ft-mse (have, 335 MB, data/hf_cache) + DINOv2-base (have). Optional SDXL VAE (have; CPU only, about 11 s/pass per 009/pilot/critique.md). Zero new download.
  - *Phase-5 reach:* the only hypothesis in docs/06 with a plausible route to AUROC >= 0.95 after laundering (if gate 2 holds, the win-rate ceiling is ~0.98). FPR <= 1% plausible with fov512 calibration. RAID/white-box robustness: unknown and likely poor (fragile-recon-arxiv2026).

- **Status:** proposed

- **Pilot result (2026-10-25, UNINFORMATIVE by pre-registration, would be FAIL; experiments/012-self-ref/pilot/, critique.md):**
  - The deployable score B (matched L0 on t and VAE(t)) is at chance: 0.533 clean / 0.496 laundered / 0.539 social in fov512, and 0.484 laundered in orig.
  - Design flaw (critic): fov512 downsizes every fake, so none is still a decoder fixed point. In orig at clean the effect is real (B 0.704; SD-family 0.867), but laundering kills it in both arms.
  - The mechanism is falsified under resampling. A blur matches it laundered, though at clean orig B beats blur by +0.368.
  - Side result: E4's pooled pair-trained F2 probe (never saw a fake) reaches 0.892 clean / 0.665 laundered / 0.759 social on fov512 unseen generators. It is not a "smooth = fake" axis (a blur-trained version transfers inverted), but it is also not decoder-specific (DALL-E 2 0.92). Candidate generator-free baseline.
