# 014-statistical-field (E5, Amendment 14) - design and results

Exploratory; does not count toward the stop rule; no method claim. Run: `env/.venv/bin/python experiments/014-statistical-field/run.py all` (stages `stats`, `tests`, `report`; resumable via `cache/`). Self-test: `selftest.py` (output `results/selftest.txt`, all PASS). No pywt in the venv: Haar (decimated) is implemented in numpy; db2 not used.

## Design (frozen in config.json before any result was computed)
- Data: all 2,919 rows of `003/splits.csv` (arm `orig`) and `004/splits_fov512.csv` (arm `fov512`); no detector is trained, so the split column is ignored. Conditions `clean`, `jpeg75_resize05`, `social_sim` via `experiments/lib/perturb.apply_condition(img, cond, key=sha256, seed=0)` (same call as the 004 DINOv2 extraction, so the statistics and DINOv2 features see identical pixels, identical for both classes).
- 38 statistics: S1 (alpha, fit residual), S2 (Haar levels 1-4 x H,V,D x log10 variance, excess kurtosis = 24), S3 (noise-level function: slope/intercept of MAD sigma and of sigma^2 vs intensity, 8 equal-count bins), S4 (corr(R-G, B-G), corr(R,G), corr(B,G), green-residual 2x2 phase CV and checkerboard contrast), S5 (patch entropy, local-contrast mean and std). Definitions in config.json.
- Atlas: Cliff's delta = 2 AUROC - 1 (positive = fake larger), reals vs each generator, cluster bootstrap (reals by device, RAISE per image, fakes by image within generator, 1,000 replicates; `lib/metrics.bootstrap_weights`). CI for the all-reals pool; real sub-pools (native, WA, FBH, RAISE) are point estimates.
- MMD: RBF, median heuristic on z-scored features, unbiased MMD^2, 1,000 label permutations (image level), Holm over 8 generators per space x arm x condition. Spaces: battery, DINOv2-B0 (block 12 CLS+mean, 1536-d), blocks 4 and 8 (block 12 is identical to B0, not repeated).
- C2ST fold scheme (device- and generator-held-out): 4 folds. Real groups (VISION device, so native/WA/FBH copies of one device stay together; each RAISE image is its own group because RAISE has no device id) and the 8 generators are shuffled with a seed and dealt round-robin into 4 folds (2 generators per fold). Test fold = its real groups + its 2 generators' fakes; training = all other groups/generators. Twin-disjoint pruning inside every fold (training fakes whose RAISE twin id is a test-fold RAISE real, and training RAISE reals whose id is a test-fold fake twin, are dropped). Logistic regression (balanced, C=1 battery / 0.01 DINOv2, train-fold z-scoring). AUROC of pooled out-of-fold scores, mean over 5 fold repeats; CI from the cluster bootstrap averaged over the repeats. A plain-fold (no pruning) sensitivity with 3 repeats is in tables.md.
- Twin analysis: pair id = regex `(r[0-9a-f]{8,}t)` on the basename of `src_path` (as in the H4 critic check c4); 796 pairs, 361 RAISE reals with at least one twin; SD2/SDXL have twins for only about 40% of ids. Paired sign tests per statistic (Holm over 38). Paired C2ST: 4 id folds x 4 generator folds (16 cells), the model never sees the test id or the test generators; AUROC averaged over cells weighted by pairs, plus the matched-pair win rate.

## Interpretations the pre-registration left open (decided before running, in config.json)
- S3 slope/intercept fitted on sigma (literal reading) and also on sigma^2 (Poisson-Gaussian form); S4 "correlation of the R-G and B-G residuals" = corr(hpR-hpG, hpB-hpG), plus the two single-pair correlations; "2x2 phase energy" = coefficient of variation and checkerboard contrast of green residual energy over the 4 pixel phases.
- Predictions evaluated on arm `orig` (primary), fov512 reported. P1 uses the twin paired C2ST of the battery (threshold on the point estimate; CI reported). P2 is read strictly: battery C2ST < 0.70 AND DINOv2-B0 C2ST < 0.70 AND MMD (battery and DINOv2-B0, all-reals pool) not significant for any of the 8 generators. P3 operationalised as P3a (>=3 of top-5 clean statistics in F = S2L1 u S3 u S4, and mean |delta| in F > rest) and P3b (min ratio of S1, S5 > max ratio of S2L1, S3, S4, ratio = laundered/clean mean |delta|). P4 = battery C2ST(orig, natives) - C2ST(fov512, WA u RAISE) >= 0.05 at `clean`.

## Results (all numbers from results/results.json and results/tables.md)
- **P1 CONFIRMED** (orig, clean): twin-paired battery C2ST AUROC 0.777 [0.754, 0.800], matched-pair win rate 0.77; fov512 0.754 [0.729, 0.779]. DINOv2-B0 on the same pairs 0.866 (orig) / 0.838 (fov512).
- **P2 REFUTED**: at jpeg75_resize05 (orig) battery C2ST 0.710 [0.676, 0.739] (just above 0.70; fov512 0.699), DINOv2-B0 C2ST 0.940 [0.924, 0.951], and MMD is significant for 8/8 generators in both spaces (Holm 0.01; permutation floor p = 0.001 reached). Laundering lowers the hand-built battery to near the 0.70 line but leaves the DINOv2 separation essentially intact (0.948 -> 0.940), so "the two populations become statistically indistinguishable" does not hold for the learned features.
- **P3 REFUTED** (orig): P3a true (top-5 clean statistics s4_corr_dRG_dBG, s2_l1_D_logvar, s4_corr_RG, s4_corr_BG, s2_l1_V_logvar are all in F; mean |delta| F 0.264 vs 0.232), P3b false: ratios jpeg75_resize05 S1 0.83, S2L1 0.76, S2L2-4 1.08, S3 0.95, S4 0.52, S5 1.00. Only S4 (colour-processing traces) is strongly suppressed; S3 is barely changed (and is weak in the first place), S2L2-4 effects are not reduced. (social_sim P3b true; fov512 P3a false, P3b false.)
- **P4 CONFIRMED** (clean): 0.972 (natives, orig) - 0.723 (WA u RAISE, fov512) = +0.249. Single factors (battery clean): orig natives 0.972, orig WA u RAISE 0.760, fov512 natives 0.743, fov512 WA 0.852, FBH 0.820, RAISE 0.741. So most of the apparent battery separability against VISION natives is scale/format, as expected. The twin result P1 shows a residual content-controlled difference remains.
- Top-5 |delta| (mean over 8 generators): clean orig s4_corr_dRG_dBG 0.52, s2_l1_D_logvar 0.41, s4_corr_RG 0.38, s4_corr_BG 0.35, s2_l1_V_logvar 0.34; jpeg75_resize05 orig s4_corr_dRG_dBG 0.35, s5_lc_mean 0.33, s2_l4_D_logvar 0.33, s2_l4_V_logvar 0.33, s5_entropy 0.31.
- C2ST battery vs DINOv2-B0 (orig; clean / jpeg75_resize05 / social_sim): battery 0.780 / 0.710 / 0.748; DINOv2-B0 0.948 / 0.940 / 0.944; blk4 0.903 / 0.902 / 0.895; blk8 0.981 / 0.970 / 0.969.
- Twin sign tests: the number of statistics significant at 0.01 (Holm, of 38) varies strongly by generator (orig clean: dalle2 19, dalle3 16, firefly 32, midjourney 5, SD2 17, SDXL 5, sd3 27, flux 10; pooled per-id mean 7); the colour-correlation statistics (S4) and level-1 Haar variances are the most frequent leaders.

## Caveats
- MMD permutations are at the image level; real WA/FBH copies are near duplicates of natives and devices cluster, so p-values are anticonservative. A sensitivity with reals = native + RAISE gives the same 8/8 significance (tables.md). Not a concern for the P2 verdict since the DINOv2 C2ST alone refutes it.
- The twin C2ST pairs a RAISE real with fakes made from a caption of that image; content is matched only semantically, not pixelwise. RAISE reals are raw-converted images (format differs from the fakes), so P1 does not exclude a RAISE-vs-synthetic format cue.
- C2ST uses a linear classifier; a non-linear battery classifier could score higher.
- The orig `native` and the fov512 arms differ in both scale and (for fakes) resampling; P4's decomposition is descriptive.
